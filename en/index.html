My approach

From knowledge to
pedagogical action.

Research is my starting point for understanding educational situations more deeply, designing conditions for learning and engaging in reflective action with teachers and students.

RESEARCHI collect data and analyse complex educational phenomena
UNDERSTANDI interpret them through theory and experience
DESIGNI create educational solutions and learning environments
ACTI implement, collaborate and support practice
REFLECTI evaluate and develop solutions in dialogue with practice
